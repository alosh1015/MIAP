Consolidated disclaimers, decluttered workspace, and model impression
Date: 2026-11-10 (Africa/Kampala)

Scope
- One safety notice per screen, rendered at the bottom by a shared
  `ScreenWarning` component. Duplicate inline disclaimers were removed from the
  dashboard, new-study, new-patient, patient banner and study workspace.
  Statements are de-duplicated by exact text and combined into a single list.
- Critical or functional notices stay inline: administrator no-pixel access,
  result-view mismatch, demo/degraded model, unavailable candidate model, and
  the encrypted review-note notice.
- Study workspace decluttered: the trust strip, viewer caption, duplicate
  de-identification notice and separate add-images expander were removed. The
  viewer and image summary form the sticky centre column at >=1280 px
  (`xl:sticky xl:top-[72px] xl:self-start`), with findings/review on the right.
- Image upload moved into a dialog opened from the compact `Images` button.
- Review is inline: Confirm/Dismiss and an optional note expand in the finding
  row. No modal opens for review; the API call is the existing
  `POST /results/{result_id}/findings/{index}/review`.
- Server-computed `impression` added to the v2 result presentation and shared by
  the UI, clinician PDF and researcher PDF:
  `{kind, label, plain_label, band, band_label, probability, text}`.
  `kind` is `finding`, `density` or `none`. A borderline leader uses
  "Possible pattern: ..." and states that the signal is weak; an empty review
  band returns the fixed no-finding sentence.
- PDFs gain an "Impression" (clinician) or "Model impression" (researcher)
  paragraph. The impression text avoids the em dash because Helvetica PDF
  extraction showed it as an unmapped glyph.
